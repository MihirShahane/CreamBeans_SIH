Cream Beans — Smart Campus Lost & Found
Cream Beans is a smart Lost & Found platform designed for college campuses.

The motive behind is simple: Eliminating the dependency on WhatsApp groups, notice boards and social media posts to find and report lost items across campuses.
Through the Cream Beans platform, students can report items that they have lost or found, along with details regarding the item like an image, a description, location and time of the item being lost or being reported. The system then looks through available reports and suggests possible matches.
